# en-US phonetic pack — EXPERIMENTAL

**Status: experimental.** Control pack (Latin script only).

## Coverage
- Digraphs: gh(∅) ph sh ch th wh ck ng qu wr kn gn dge; length-insensitive
  vowels: igh ee ea oo ou ow ai ay oi oy au aw ei ey ie ue ui.
- Grammar: wake (hey/alexa), verbs (play/queue/shuffle/find), providers
  (spotify/pandora/youtube…), fillers (please/could/song…), intents
  live/remix/acoustic/original + by-artist.
- 12 fixtures incl. mangled "blinding lites", "nite fever", "rhapsoddy",
  apostrophe handling "don't stop me now".

## Known gaps (honest)
- Articles ("the","a","me") deliberately not stripped (protects titles);
  gh→∅ is blunt (no "gh as g" context); y classed as consonant; no stress/
  vowel-reduction model (photograph/photography not linked).
