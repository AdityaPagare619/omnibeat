2. **Sources for that arithmetic:** aws.amazon.com/transcribe/pricing (streaming $0.01/min), aws.amazon.com/polly/pricing (Neural $16/M chars, marks billed per char), builder.aws.com Bedrock cheat sheet for the Nova rates — the last is PARTIAL because AWS's own Bedrock pricing page serves only JS-resolved `{priceOf!…}` placeholders (CLM-AWS-021).
